About My Hobby:
Rock Climbing.

I started Rock Climbing in 2020. Rock climbing not only trains the body, but also challenges the mind. The sport requires problem-solving - figuring out different ways to approach a route, and also physically - requiring strength and endurance. Rock climbing has really taught me to be patient, and to be persistent. I have learned that it is okay to fail and to try again, and that it is okay to ask for help.

Why I love the sport

Having started rock climbing at a young age, I truly feel like I grew into the sport. It shaped how I look at challenges. On the wall, everything becomes a puzzle. I love the mental game of mapping out a route, calculating exactly where to place my feet and how to grip each hold. The climbing community is also very supportive and welcoming, through which I have made many friends.

Safety precautions

Safety is number one priority in climbing. I always make sure that the rope is tied tight, and that the belayer is paying attention. And when I am bouldering, I always make sure that I look where I am landing before jumping.

Where I Climb

I climb regularly at Ground Up Climbing Gym. I enjoy climbing here because not only does it have top rope climbing, but also bouldering, which gives me the option to do both types of climbing without having to go to different gyms. The monthly membership is also very affordable compared to other rock climbing gyms
