Why I Ride

About My Hobby:
Cycling.

I ride mostly because it's fun. There's nothing quite like being out on the road with the wind in your face and no real destination in mind. It started as a way to stay active without dreading it. I also look forward to cycling in the morning to see the sunrise.

How I started riding

I started cycling many years ago, but in 2024 I decided to invest in a road bike. I bought my first road bike off of Carousel for $500, and it lasted me for slightly more than a year before I decided to buy a new bike. Before I started riding road bikes, I was riding mountain bikes. Both types of bikes have a very different feel, however I definitely enjoy riding road bikes more.
